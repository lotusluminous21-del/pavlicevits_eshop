# 01 — Brand Manifesto

**Pavlicevits Colors**
*Kalamaria, Thessaloniki — Since 1990*

---

## Why this document exists

This is the compass. Every visual, content, or product decision can be checked against the principles here. If a draft passes, ship it. If it doesn't, edit until it does.

It's also the first thing every external partner — design crew, photographers, copywriters, agencies — should read before touching the brand.

---

## 1. Who we are

Pavlicevits Colors is a paint specialist in Kalamaria, Thessaloniki, founded in 1990 with trade roots back to 1982 when the family represented a German paint manufacturer in Northern Greece. Two generations of the same family work the counter today.

We work in four product categories — and only four:

- **Architectural and decorative paints** — interior, exterior, premium and standard.
- **Automotive refinish paints** — for body shops and individual restoration.
- **Marine paints** — antifouling, primers, topcoats for yachts and commercial vessels.
- **Special applications** — industrial floors, pools, metal structures, custom color formulation.

We are not a generic paint store. We are not the cheapest shop. We don't try to be everything to everyone. We are the technical partner you call when the job needs more than "any paint in any color."

We hold an exclusive cooperation with **Pellachrom**, a Greek paint manufacturer in Edessa (Pella regional unit, Northern Greece, ~100 km from us). Beyond Pellachrom, we stock the leading Greek and international brands — Vivechrom, Vechro, Kraft, Vitex, Isomat — chosen and curated, not warehoused.

## 2. Where we come from

In 1982 the family began representing German paint products in Northern Greece — learning that quality is a language you either speak or you don't. In 1990 we took our own name. From 2002 onwards we expanded systematically into automotive, marine, industrial, and special-application paints, becoming the address professionals and architects in Northern Greece return to when "any paint" isn't enough.

The same standards have run through that timeline. The tools change. The standards don't.

Today the next generation drives the brand and the digital transformation. The previous generation still runs the counter. Both work side by side. The handoff is not a break; it's a continuation. We don't say "new generation, new era" — we let the work say it.

## 3. What we believe

### Discipline beats display.

We don't shout on social. We don't promise what we can't deliver. We don't chase trends that don't fit. We don't run discounts to fill gaps. We don't exist to convince — we exist to be found by people who are already looking for what we offer.

### The relationship is the product.

The paint is the deliverable. The advice is the real service. The relationship is the actual product. Customers who came to us in 1995 still come to us. Their kids come to us. That's not marketing — it's the consequence of doing the job right, decade after decade.

### The right solution, not just the available one.

Internal mantra. Every recommendation we give is the one we'd use ourselves on our own job — not the one with the highest margin, not the one we want to clear. The job has to outlast the conversation.

### Color is transformation.

Paint isn't decorative — it's transformative. A whitewashed wall isn't "the same wall, painted." It's a different room. A re-coated yacht hull isn't "freshly painted" — it's a different vessel, ready for another five years at sea. We're in the business of transformation. The slogan we use everywhere — **"Making Your Life Colorful"** — is not aesthetic copy. It's a promise: the color you leave with from us will change your space, your job, your asset. We mean it literally.

### Material quality is non-negotiable.

The single most important thing on our shelves is what's in the can. Pigment density. Resin chemistry. Lightfastness. Coverage. Drying time. Salt resistance. Chemical resistance. Adhesion to substrate. These are not abstract specs — they are the difference between a hotel facade that lasts seven years and one that lasts two. We refuse to carry, recommend, or discount our way into materials that don't meet our standards. This is the most concrete promise we make.

## 4. What we actually sell — and why it differs from what generic shops sell

The brand narrative and personality are the entry points. Material quality is the substance behind them. Both must be felt by anyone who interacts with us — visitor on the website, customer at the counter, architect on a project call.

### 4.1 We curate. We don't warehouse.

A generic paint store stocks every brand and every product. We don't. We carry brands and lines we have personally tested, used on our own work or on customer projects we know intimately, and would recommend to a friend without hesitation. If a product doesn't meet that bar, it doesn't enter our shelves — regardless of margin, regardless of supplier pressure.

### 4.2 The Pellachrom partnership — depth, not logo

Our cooperation with Pellachrom (D.G.K. Pellachrom, Edessa, founded 1981) is not a distribution arrangement. It's a working relationship that gives us:

- **Direct technical access** to Pellachrom's formulators when a job has unusual substrate or environmental conditions.
- **Custom formulation capability** for orders that don't fit catalog products — non-standard hues, modified resin systems for specific exposure conditions, batch consistency for multi-phase projects.
- **Northern-Greek manufacturing proximity** (~100 km from our store) — which means short supply chains, fresh stock, faster custom-batch turnaround, and a real-time partnership rather than a distributor tier.

### 4.3 Beyond Pellachrom — the curated portfolio

We additionally stock the leading Greek and international brands. Each one is on our shelves for a specific reason:

- **Vivechrom** (AkzoNobel — Greek market leader since 1964): for proven decorative systems with deep technical documentation and broad professional acceptance.
- **Vechro** (Greek-owned, eco-paint leader with 96 certified products): for projects where eco-certification is required or preferred — independent, third-party verification, not greenwashing.
- **Kraft Paints** (Druckfarben group, 900+ sales points nationally): for value-tier solutions where the price-quality balance must be precise.
- **Vitex, Isomat**, and others: for specialty cases where their lines are best-in-category.

We don't double-stock. If two brands offer near-identical products, we carry the one we've found more reliable in the field — not both for "choice."

### 4.4 The technical truths we apply when stocking

When we evaluate whether a product earns shelf space, we look at — in this order:

1. **Performance under real conditions**, not lab spec sheets. We use it ourselves or watch trusted painters use it.
2. **Pigment integrity over time** — does the color hold at year five, or fade by year two?
3. **Coverage and yield** — does the listed yield match field reality?
4. **Substrate compatibility** — does it adhere properly across the range of substrates our customers actually face?
5. **Documented technical support** — datasheets, MSDS, manufacturer responsiveness when there's a question.
6. **Safety and certification** — VOC compliance, eco-certifications where claimed.

If a product fails any of these in our experience, it's removed.

### 4.5 What this means for the customer

When you walk in, the recommendation you receive is the recommendation we'd give a peer. The brand on the can matters less than the certainty that what's inside the can will perform. The price you see reflects the quality, not warehouse-clearance pressure. The advice you get is the advice that would protect your job from re-painting, re-coating, or warranty failure.

This is why our customers come back — and why their projects last.

## 5. Two faces, one person — the brand's expression duality

The next-generation owner who drives this rebrand carries two distinct aesthetic registers:

| Personal — Shadow side | Brand — Light side |
|---|---|
| Cinematic, monochrome, motivational, lone-wolf | Vibrant, theatrical, deliberate, professional |
| "I owe it to myself" — discipline as faith | "Making your life colorful" — color as gift |
| Curated quotes, romantic-noir, internal | Dramatic photography, glowing color, public |
| Doesn't show, doesn't explain | Shows everything, with editorial restraint |

These aren't contradictions. They're the same person in two registers. The shadow side is the discipline. The light side is what that discipline produces. **The brand lives almost entirely in the light side** — but the silent intensity of the shadow side is the pilot light beneath it. Visitors feel it without naming it.

Practically: our brand expression is **dramatic, vibrant, photo-led, theatrical with control** — the disciplined showmanship of someone who has earned the right to be theatrical. The visual direction (document 02) inherits this directly.

## 6. The slogan

> ## Making Your Life Colorful.

Always English. Never translated. Never replaced with a tagline. It is the brand's signature.

The reason it works: it is performative. Every time someone leaves our shop with a sample matched to spec, with a recommendation that saves them from re-painting in two years, with a project that lasts seven — that's an act of "making life colorful." Not a slogan. A unit of work delivered.

For headers, the slogan often appears stand-alone, italicized, in our signature color (petrol). For body or footer use, it sits as a sub-line under the wordmark.

## 7. The audience priority — explicit ranking

We design and write for these audiences in this order. Where attention or budget must trade off, the higher rank wins.

| # | Audience | Job they want done |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Professional painters & contractors (especially millennial / Gen-Z entering decision-making roles) | "Find me a supplier who treats me as a peer and gets the technical answer right the first time." |
| 2 | Architects, designers, hotel project managers, marine professionals | "Find me a partner who can specify systems that won't fail under coastal exposure / industrial use / heritage substrates." |
| 3 | Discerning homeowners (mid-to-high-end) — Kalamaria, Pylaia, Panorama, Thermi catchment | "Find me an expert who'll tell me the truth about what my walls actually need." |
| 4 | DIY / mass-market homeowners | Served excellently in store. Not pursued through paid marketing. |

**The brand voice is calibrated to Tier 1.** When Tier 1 trusts us, Tiers 2-3 follow. Tier 4 receives us through walk-in and word-of-mouth.

## 8. The geography

- **Year 1 reach**: Northern Greece — Thessaloniki, Halkidiki, broader Macedonia.
- **Year 2-3**: greater Thessaloniki overflow, with selective national reach in marine and custom-formulation.
- **Vision (long-term)**: nationwide for specialty work; never compete on price-led generic decor.

The Halkidiki hospitality construction wave (over €300m in announced projects with named developers — Goldman Sachs, Leonardo Hotels, Mitsis), Northern Greece's marine sector (Porto Carras, Sani — combined ~530 berths), and the affluent Kalamaria/east-Thessaloniki residential market are our three concrete demand pools.

## 9. Voice

Five principles, in priority order:

1. **Greek-first in the market, English-ready in tone.** The site, social, and direct comms are in Greek. The brand vocabulary (slogan, brand name, key marks) lives in English where the founder set it. International audiences (marina clientele, foreign hotel project managers) get English variants.
2. **Plain technical.** Use the proper term and immediately explain what it means. Never bluff. Never patronize.
3. **Precise, not promotional.** "Exact match in sample" beats "Premium quality color matching." Specifics carry credibility; superlatives do the opposite.
4. **Warm, not soft.** Address the reader directly. We're not customer-service-friendly; we're peer-to-peer-friendly.
5. **Calm.** We're the steady voice in a noisy renovation. We never shout, never scare, never overpromise.

## 10. The "no" list

Identity is defined as much by what we refuse to do as by what we do.

- We don't run discount banners.
- We don't auto-play sliders or pop-up newsletter forms.
- We don't post motivational quotes on the brand account.
- We don't write "Click here," "Buy now," "Get started," or "Sign up."
- We don't use stock photography.
- We don't claim what we can't prove.
- We don't market to Tier 4 with paid attention.
- We don't translate the slogan.
- We don't compete head-to-head with Kosmaoglou on premium decorative.
- We don't dilute the brand to fit a trend.

## 11. The truth beneath everything

**I owe it to myself.**

Not a tagline. The way we operate.

We don't owe anyone the version of us that fits their convenience. We owe ourselves the version of us we can be, doing this thing as well as it can be done. Color is the most ambitious thing a wall can do. We sit at the meeting point of color science, technical paint, Northern-Greek heritage, and contemporary craft. From here we work — as Greeks, from Kalamaria, with knowledge, with style, with discipline, with heart.

This document doesn't go on banners. It stays as the compass.
